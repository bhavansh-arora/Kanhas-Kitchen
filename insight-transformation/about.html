(01) — My missionFor women ready to act

In taking authentic action, women find their inner voice and the strength to know that they are more than enough.

Alberta McKnight · Founder & coach
(02) — My approachRooted in ontology

Coaching for who you are — and who you’re becoming.

My coach training is based on ontology — the philosophical study of the nature of being, becoming, existence and reality.

My focus is to support you in taking your vision — your wishing and wanting — to reality. The coaching experience centers on three characteristics, and the journey is about creating the life you are meant to live: with clarity, focus, ease and grace.

  • Existence → KnowledgeKnowing where you are and what you wantLevel I
  • Consciousness → AwarenessSeeing the patterns that stop youLevel II
  • Bliss → JoyLiving the life you’re meant to liveLevel III
(03) — How I coachFour promises

Clarity, focus, ease & grace.

Whatever level you choose, these four qualities shape every conversation we have.

Clarity

We clear the fog so you can see what you truly want — and what has been in the way.

Focus

We shift your attention away from what has no meaning and toward what does.

Ease

Momentum without the struggle. Action that feels like yours, not a chore.

Grace

Kindness for yourself as you grow. You are already more than enough.

My promise to you
“As your coach, I will walk alongside you and, where there are blocks along the road, support you in clearing the path to attain your goal — and, ultimately, your joy.”
