The ideas that I think matter most for turning visitors into booked calls:

**1. Your packages, side by side.** Level I (Knowledge), Level II (Awareness) and Level III (Joy) are shown together, with what each includes and a comparison table. Visitors can see how the levels build on each other and place themselves before they reach out.

**2. A "Find your level" quiz.** Five quick questions recommend a starting level. The answers, including what she wants help with and whether she prefers video or phone, carry straight into the booking form. You hear from women who have already thought about where they are, which means fewer low-quality inquiries.

**3. A booking form that screens for you.** Your "I am interested in" options are now step one of a short three-step request. By the time a message reaches you, you know her focus, her likely level and how she'd like to meet. It can send to your inbox, or connect to a scheduler like Calendly whenever you're ready.

**4. One clear next step, everywhere.** Every page leads to your free get-to-know-you session. On phones, a small booking bar follows the visitor down the page, so booking is always one tap away.

**5. Your philosophy, made visible.** Your ontology training is what sets you apart, so the site shows it: existence, consciousness and bliss become three overlapping circles on the About page, and Knowledge, Awareness and Joy unfold as a journey "from fog to light" on the home page. **6. Louisa and Rebecca, front and center.** Their stories get real space as large pull-quotes, with each full testimonial one tap away.
