Six clear steps, handled by one team. At every stage you’ll know what’s happening, what you need to do (usually nothing), and what comes next.

Track a project
01

Consultation

Typical: day 1You: share a recent bill

Needs evaluation

An in-depth conversation about your energy needs, budget and solar goals.

Site assessment

We visit the property to confirm its solar potential and the space available.

Tailored proposal

A detailed proposal with system options, expected savings and financing.

02

Engineering & design

Typical: 1–2 weeksYou: approve the design

System design

A custom design that maximizes efficiency, follows local regulations and respects how your property looks.

Technical specifications

Detailed specs for every component going into your system.

Project schedule

A clear schedule so you’re updated at every stage of installation.

03

Permitting

Typical: varies by cityYou: nothing

Documentation

We prepare all the paperwork needed for local permits and approvals.

Code compliance

Designs are checked against local building standards to reduce approval delays.

Permit application

We submit to local authorities and keep you informed until it’s approved.

04

Installation

Typical: 1–3 daysYou: provide access

Expert installation

Qualified technicians install your system to industry standards for safety and performance.

Quality assurance

Checks throughout the install confirm every component is fitted correctly.

Open communication

The crew answers your questions as they go. No guessing what’s happening on your roof.

05

Inspection

Typical: after installYou: nothing

Post-install evaluation

A thorough check that the system performs properly and meets safety regulations.

Utility coordination

We work with your utility on any inspections or authorizations before activation.

Final tuning

Adjustments based on inspection feedback to get the best performance.

06

Permission to operate

The finish lineYou: flip the switch with us

Final approval

Final confirmation from the authorities that your system can operate.

System activation

We help you switch on and start producing your own clean power.

Continuous support

Ongoing monitoring and support to keep your system running efficiently.
