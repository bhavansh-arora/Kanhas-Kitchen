(02)Why storage matters now

Your solar should work after dark, too.

Since April 2023, new solar customers of California’s major utilities are on the Net Billing Tariff (often called NEM 3.0), which pays less for the power you send back. Storing your own solar for the evening is how you keep more of its value.
