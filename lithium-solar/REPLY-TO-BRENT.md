**The five ideas**

1. **One quote path.** Right now your two "Get a Quote" buttons go to different places (one to a third-party form, one to the Contact page), so requests land in two places and are hard to track. In the preview, every button leads to one short quote flow.

2. **Show the value before asking for details.** Visitors slide their monthly bill and see their estimated system size, payback and 25-year savings on step 2, before we ask for a name or phone number. People who have seen their own number are far more likely to finish.

3. **Book the site assessment inside the quote.** Step 6 lets them pick a day and time window for the free visit, and the confirmation adds it to their calendar. The request reaches you as a booked assessment, not a cold lead to chase.

4. **Leads that arrive prepared.** Each request comes with the bill, goals (backup, EV, panel upgrade), ownership, roof shade, timeline and address, so your consultant walks in ready. Answers save as they go, so anyone who leaves halfway can pick up where they stopped.

5. **No project goes quiet.** Every request gets a tracking code. Customers can check their stage, from consultation through permission to operate, any time. That should mean fewer "any update?" calls while permits are pending.

We also fixed a few small things that cost trust: a second phone number, +88 (9800) 6802, that looks like a template leftover; "Consulation" and "Commerical" typos on buttons; and blog posts that read like template placeholders. The whole site also works properly on phones, with Call and Free Quote buttons always within thumb reach.
