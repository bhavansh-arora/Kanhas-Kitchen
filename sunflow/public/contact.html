Yes. The consultation, roof inspection and proposal are free, with no obligation.

We're based in Barrie and work across Simcoe County, the Greater Toronto Area and cottage country, and we install commercial solar in Manitoba. If you're elsewhere, ask — we'll tell you honestly if we can help.

What should I have ready?

For solar, a recent hydro bill helps us size the system. For roofing and exteriors, a few photos and the roof's approximate age are plenty.
