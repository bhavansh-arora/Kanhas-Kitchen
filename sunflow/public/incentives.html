      (04) — Questions

      Straight answers.

      Do I have to pay for the whole system upfront?

      No. Businesses can finance and deduct the interest, and municipalities and non-profits can use a power purchase agreement to go solar with no upfront capital. We model each option in your proposal.

      How long will these incentives last?

      Several are limited-time, including the federal investment tax credit, and grant funding windows open and close. Waiting can mean missing out — which is why we check what's available for you right now.

      Does solar really work through a Canadian winter?

      Yes. Our systems are built to be resilient through Canadian winters, and net metering lets you bank summer surplus as credit for up to 12 months to offset darker months.

      Can my solar pay for a new roof?

      Often, yes. We're flat-roof and metal-roof specialists, and every solar consultation includes a free roof inspection. If the roof needs work, we build it into the plan so your savings help cover it.
