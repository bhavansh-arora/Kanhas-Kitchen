Mike McCann

Founder & CEO, SunFlow · Founder, Love Barrie & Simcoe County

Mike built SunFlow on a simple belief: people deserve contractors who respect and care for both their clients and the quality of their work.

He's on site at the company's most demanding projects — from zinc at the Spadina Museum with CR Systems' Karl Hakkarainen to commercial arrays for local businesses — and off site, he's the founder of Love Barrie & Simcoe County, bringing the community together for causes that matter.
