The quote form is the part to try first: Free quote, top right on any page.

**What we noticed on the current site**

- Every "Book a Consultation" button opens the same monday.com form, whatever page the visitor was on. A roofing repair and a $1M commercial solar enquiry arrive looking the same, so someone has to sort them by hand.
- The contact page is built around booking a solar call. Roofing, insulation and luxury exterior enquiries don't have their own way in.
- Your strongest proof is on inner pages: the $924K project paid off in seven years, the 47–72% first-year returns, the Spadina Museum work. Many visitors decide whether to get in touch before they ever reach those pages.

**What we built to fix that**

1. **A quote form that asks the right questions.** Five short steps, about two minutes. It changes with the service: hydro bill and roof type for solar, what's wrong with the roof for roofing, project stage and architect for luxury exteriors, warning signs like ice dams for insulation.
2. **Every request arrives sorted.** Each one is tagged with service, province, property type, timeline and budget, and marked Hot, Warm or Nurture. It goes straight to the right person. As a starting point, commercial solar and luxury exteriors go to Mike, and residential solar, roofing and insulation come to you. That's one line to change.
3. **It works with what you already use.** Requests can land on your existing monday.com board as new items with every field filled in, plus an email alert. Your team's process doesn't change.
4. **Fewer leads lost.** If someone closes the tab halfway, their answers are still there when they come back. Each request also records where the visitor came from (Google, Facebook, a referral, a campaign), so you can see which marketing actually brings in jobs.
5. **Tools that turn browsers into enquiries.** A year-one incentive calculator (Ontario vs Manitoba, built from your 2025 reference proposals) passes its numbers straight into the quote form. There's also a six-question incentive eligibility checker, and an R8-to-R60 attic slider on the insulation page.
6. **A clear next step everywhere.** Every page ends with one, and on phones a "Call / Get my free quote" bar stays within thumb's reach.

**What it means for you day to day**

- Less time on qualifying calls, because you already know the service, location, size and timing before you pick up the phone.
- Your biggest opportunities, like commercial and institutional work, are flagged and reach Mike first.
- Nothing slips through a shared inbox.
- You can see which channels bring real jobs, not just clicks.

The rest of the site got the same care: every page redesigned, fast on phones and tablets, using your own content, photos and figures. It includes case studies for the Spadina Museum and the downtown Toronto residence, and your community work with Love Barrie.

Would a 20-minute call this week work to walk you and Mike through it? If you like it, the next step is connecting the form to your monday.com board and agreeing who gets which requests.
