Commercial FAQ

The fine print.

Want a site visit? Request a commercial proposal.

Who can apply for the SEAI microgen grant?

Businesses, farms, schools, community centres, public sector bodies and non-profit societies. The installer must be SEAI-registered, and the grant offer must be in place before any work starts.

Roof or ground mount?

We design and install both. Roof mounts use space you already have; ground mounts suit sites with land available or roofs that can't take the load. We'll recommend the best option after the survey.

Will installation disrupt my business?

We plan installation around your operations and keep downtime for the grid connection to a minimum, scheduled at a time that suits you.

Can we track savings for ESG reporting?

Yes. Every system includes monitoring, so you can see generation, self-consumption and export in real time and export the data for reporting.
