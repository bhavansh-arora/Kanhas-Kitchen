Good questions

Straight answers.

Still wondering? Ring us on (057) 875 6595. You'll talk to the people who design and install your system.

Does solar actually work on cloudy Irish days?

Yes. Panels generate from daylight, not just direct sun, so they still produce on overcast days. A well-designed system in the Irish midlands typically generates around 850–950 kWh per kWp every year.

How much is the SEAI solar grant for my home?

€700 per kWp for the first 2 kWp and €200 per kWp for the next 2 kWp, up to €1,800, plus €600 for an eligible battery of 5 kWh or more. Your home must have been built and occupied before 2025, and the grant must be approved before work begins. We handle the application for you.

Do I need planning permission?

Most rooftop systems on Irish homes are exempt from planning permission. Exceptions can apply to protected structures, architectural conservation areas and some locations near airports. We check your site as part of the design.

Is a battery worth adding?

A battery stores the power you don't use during the day so you can use it in the evening peak, when electricity is most expensive. It also unlocks an extra €600 SEAI grant. Depending on system size, solar plus storage can meet up to 100% of a home's energy needs.

Can I get paid for the power I export?

Yes. Under the Clean Export Guarantee, your electricity supplier pays you for surplus power your system sends back to the grid. We set your system up to make the most of it.

What happens after installation?

You get live monitoring on your phone, a 30-year product and performance warranty on panels, and dedicated aftercare with priority support from our team in Co. Laois.
