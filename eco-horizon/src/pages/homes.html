Grant, handled

We do the paperwork.

The SEAI grant must be approved before any work begins. Miss a step and you could lose it. We run the whole process for you.

Full grant guide
  1. 01

    Eligibility check

    Homeowner or private landlord, home built and occupied before 2025, no previous solar PV grant on your MPRN. We confirm it in your first call.

  2. 02

    Application filed

    We prepare and submit your SEAI application with the system design. No work starts until your grant offer is approved.

  3. 03

    Installation

    Our certified crew installs your system well within the 8-month grant window, with 0% VAT on qualifying residential installs.

  4. 04

    BER & payment

    A post-installation BER is completed, the claim goes in, and the grant is paid. Social welfare recipients may qualify for enhanced grants of up to €8,400 for PV, or €9,000 with battery storage.

Home solar FAQ

Before you ask.

Anything else? Call (057) 875 6595 or request a callback.

Will solar work on my roof?

South, east and west-facing roofs all work well in Ireland. Even north-facing roofs can be viable with the right layout. We assess orientation, pitch and shading during your free consultation.

Can I add a battery or EV charger later?

Yes. Our inverters and smart energy controllers are future-ready, so batteries and EV chargers can be added without starting again. Adding a battery now does unlock the extra €600 SEAI grant.

Are finance options available?

Yes. Finance options are available so you can spread the cost and let your savings help pay for the system. Ask us for details when we send your quote.

What maintenance does a system need?

Very little. Irish rain keeps panels clean, and your monitoring app flags anything unusual. If something needs a look, our aftercare team is on hand with priority support.
