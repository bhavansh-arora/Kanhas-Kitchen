Two routes to funding

TAMS 3 or SEAI? We'll pick the winner.

The two grants can't be claimed for the same investment. We model both and recommend the one that puts more back in your pocket.

TAMS 3 Solar Capital InvestmentSEAI Non-Domestic Microgen
Grant rate60% of eligible investmentBanded, up to 45% of eligible cost
Maximum grant€54,000 (on €90,000 investment)€325,200 (enhanced scheme)
System sizeUp to 62 kWp, rooftop or ground1 kWp to 1 MWp
Battery storageEligible, up to 50% of PV capacityNot covered
LocationFarm buildings or ground. Not on the farmhouseFarm, business or community buildings
SelectionRanking & selection, subject to Department approvalOnline application, offer before works
Also neededFarm energy survey by a registered installerSEAI-registered installer

The Pig & Poultry Investment Scheme has a separate 40% grant rate. Solar PV must be designed primarily for on-farm self-consumption.
