FAQ

Questions, answered

Will it make things up?

It's built not to. It answers only from what you've given it: your website, files and notes. When something isn't covered, it says so honestly and offers to have your team get back to the visitor. You can read every conversation and fix any answer by updating your knowledge.

Do my visitors need to install anything?

No. It runs right on your website. Visitors tap the chat button, then type or tap the mic. Voice works in Chrome, Edge and Safari, on phones too. In other browsers visitors can type and still hear the replies.

Which languages does it speak?

It replies in whatever language the visitor uses, including English, Hindi, Hinglish, Marathi, Gujarati, Punjabi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Arabic, Spanish, French and German. You choose the main language for listening and speaking.

How does it learn about my business?

Three ways: it reads your website, it reads files you upload (PDFs, photos, Word and PowerPoint files, spreadsheets saved as CSV), and your setup assistant interviews you in plain chat. Everything it knows is listed in your dashboard, where you can edit it.

Will it work with my website builder?

Yes. If your site lets you add a line of code (WordPress, Shopify, Wix, Squarespace, Webflow, Google Tag Manager or plain HTML), it works. On Growth and Pro we'll install it for you.

What happens to the leads?

When a visitor shares their name and phone number or email, it's saved to your dashboard with a note on what they wanted. Mark leads as contacted, won or lost, and export them as a CSV any time.

What counts as a message?

Each question a visitor asks is one message, whether typed or spoken. The assistant's replies and your own training chats don't count.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Plans are month to month unless you choose yearly billing. You can also delete your assistant's knowledge and conversations whenever you like.
