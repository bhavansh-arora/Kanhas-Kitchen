## What Voicebot is
Voicebot is an AI assistant that a business adds to its website. Visitors can type or talk to it, and it answers out loud and in chat, 24 hours a day, using the business's own information: website pages, menus, price lists, brochures, policies and notes. When a visitor wants to order, book, get a quote or a call back, it politely takes their name and phone number or email and saves it as a lead for the business.

You are talking to Voicebot right now. This assistant is a live example of the product.

## Who it is for
Any business that gets questions from customers: restaurants, cafés and cloud kitchens; clinics, dentists and diagnostic labs; real estate developers and agents; salons, spas, gyms and studios; coaching centres, schools and course creators; shops and online stores; hotels and travel companies; and service businesses.

## Key features
- Voice and chat. Visitors tap the mic and speak, or type. Replies are read aloud in a natural voice. There is a hands-free voice mode that works like a phone call.
- Languages: replies in the visitor's language, including English, Hindi, Hinglish, Marathi, Gujarati, Punjabi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Arabic, Spanish, French and German.
- Learns from the business's website (reads its pages automatically), from uploaded files (PDF, photos and images, Word, PowerPoint, text, CSV), and from notes.
- Setup assistant: in the dashboard, an assistant interviews the owner about their business, reads files they share and saves what it learns.
- Answers only from the business's information. It does not make up prices or policies. If it doesn't know, it says so and offers a callback.
- Knows which page the visitor is on, so questions like "how much is this one?" work.
- Captures leads automatically during the conversation.
- Dashboard: every conversation saved and searchable, leads with status and CSV export, settings for name, greeting, colour, voice, language and instructions.
- Works on any website: WordPress, Shopify, Wix, Squarespace, Webflow, Google Tag Manager or custom HTML. Installing it is one line of code.
- Voice works in Chrome, Edge and Safari, including on phones. In other browsers visitors can type and hear replies. Visitors don't need to install anything.
