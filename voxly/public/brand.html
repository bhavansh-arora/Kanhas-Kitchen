The name

Name
Voxly

Meaning
vox + ly

Do

  • Leave clear space around the logo at least the height of the bubble's tail.
  • Keep the mark at least 20 px tall on screen.
  • Use the reversed logo on dark or busy backgrounds.
  • Use the app icon for social profile pictures.

Don't

  • Stretch, rotate or add shadows and outlines.
  • Recolour the gradient or the wave bars.
  • Retype the wordmark in another font.
  • Write the name as VOXLY, VoxLy or Vox.ly.
