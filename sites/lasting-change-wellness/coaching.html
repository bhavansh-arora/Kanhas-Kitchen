Questions

Before you book

What happens on the free Power Up call?

It's a relaxed 30-minute video conversation. We talk about what's going on, what you'd like to be different, and what's been in the way. If coaching looks like a good fit, Lauren will recommend the option that suits you. If it isn't, she'll say so and point you toward something that might help.

How is coaching different from therapy?

Therapy often focuses on healing the past. Coaching focuses on where you are now and where you want to go, with practical steps and accountability. Lauren's background as a psychologist and family therapist means she understands the patterns behind self-doubt, but coaching is not a substitute for mental health treatment.

Is coaching in person or online?

All one-on-one coaching happens by video, so you can work with Lauren from anywhere in the world.

How soon will I notice a difference?

Many clients feel lighter after the first few sessions. Lasting change in mindset and habits takes longer, which is why packages start at three months.

What does a package cost?

A single Clarity Power Session is $350. Package pricing depends on length and focus, and Lauren shares it on your free call once you both know what fits.

I'm not ready to commit. Where should I start?

Download the free Expect Amazing Mindset Reset workbook, or book a single Clarity Power Session to experience Lauren's approach first.
