(03) — Is this you?Seven quiet signs

Regardless of your success, is this you?

If any of these are true for you, it may be a sign that you haven't yet mastered your personal power and potential. And if you want things to be different, it's time to do different things.

01

Does self-doubt continue to nag at you, like you're not enough in some way, or that things might fall apart if you loosen your grip?

02

Are you exhausted from feeling uncertain about your decisions and the weight of your responsibilities?

03

Do you struggle with impostor syndrome, or use words like “I'm not ready yet” or “Who am I to…”?

04

Does fear or guilt hold you back from going after your dreams and building the work and life you want?

05

Do other people's words or reactions sometimes make you feel small, or dim your light?

06

Are you worried about what will happen if you fall apart from doing too much all the time?

07

Do you know you're meant to have a much greater impact, but worry that you're not worthy of being out front or leading the way?

If any of these are true for you

It's time to do different things.

Swipe to see all seven→

The good news?

You can develop the mindset and habits it takes to shine your brightest.

Step by step, action by action, thought by thought. Everything you want is on the other side of what you haven't done… yet. We can get there together.

Imagine what you can achieve by…
  • Having your energy attract the relationships and outcomes you want, because you are shining with inner radiance
  • Changing your self-talk from critical to supportive, action-oriented and compassionate
  • Feeling unshakeable self-respect, because you're honoring yourself body, mind, heart and spirit
  • Approaching each moment empowered, confident and happy

(05) — The S.H.I.N.E. Method

Five steps to a self-trust that lasts.

S.H.I.N.E. fast-tracks you to design a life you love with intention, choice, and action. Keep scrolling, and watch the room get brighter.

Scroll →
Step 01 / 05See
S

See yourself

You are beautifully complicated and perfectly imperfect. Accept ALL of who you are with love.

Remember

Your strengths and your quirks are what make you unique.

Step 02 / 05Honor
H

Honor yourself

Take the best care of your body, your mind, and your spirit. Develop a positive, optimistic mindset, and create lasting healthy habits.

Remember

You deserve all the good things life offers.

Step 03 / 05Investigate
I

Investigate the possibilities

Get curious! What makes you shine? What do you want more of in your life? What is important to you?

Ask yourself

What would it look like to create a life you love to live?

Step 04 / 05Neutralize
N

Neutralize the negative

Limit anything, or the impact of anyone, that dims your light, asks you to play small, or steals your energy.

Remember

Release negative beliefs and barriers.

Step 05 / 05Elevate
E

Elevate your energy

Lead with love. Become a source of positive energy for yourself and others.

Remember

When you shine, you light up the world around you!

When you shine, you light up your world.
