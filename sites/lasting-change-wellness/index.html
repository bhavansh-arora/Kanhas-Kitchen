The S.H.I.N.E. method

Five steps to see, treat and lead yourself differently

Built on Lauren's training as a psychologist and family therapist, S.H.I.N.E. turns insight into habits, so the change doesn't fade a few weeks after the session ends.

Step 1 of 5

See yourself

Accept your whole self with compassion. Your strengths and your quirks are what make you uniquely you.

In our sessions
We look at the stories you tell about yourself, where they came from, and which ones are still true.
What shifts
You stop arguing with who you are and start working with it.

Step 2 of 5

Honor yourself

Care for your body, mind and spirit. Build an optimistic outlook and healthy habits that last.

In our sessions
We build small, realistic routines that protect your energy, and plan for the weeks when life gets in the way.
What shifts
Taking care of yourself stops being the first thing you drop.

Step 3 of 5

Investigate the possibilities

Get curious. What makes you shine? What do you want more of? What would a life you love look like?

In our sessions
We get specific about the goals you've been shelving and what “amazing” would look like for you.
What shifts
You have a clear picture of what you're working toward, and why it matters.

Step 4 of 5

Neutralize the negative

Limit the people and things that dim your light or drain your energy, and release the beliefs that keep you small.

In our sessions
We set boundaries you can actually keep, at work and at home, and practise the conversations that go with them.
What shifts
Less of your energy goes to what drains you, and more goes to you.

Step 5 of 5

Elevate your energy

Lead with love, and become a source of positive energy for yourself and the people around you.

In our sessions
We turn new mindsets into daily practice so they hold up under pressure.
What shifts
Confidence shows up on its own, without forcing it.

Meet Lauren

“You are always in the process of becoming.”

For years I hid behind a confident-looking mask. I put everyone else first and quietly doubted I deserved success.

At 53, I decided to make myself a priority. I let go of the beliefs that kept me small, started saying yes to what I love, and began caring for my body, mind and spirit. People noticed. One friend told me, “You are a light in this world.”

That shift became S.H.I.N.E. and the Expect Amazing approach. Today I bring my training as a psychologist and family therapist, and my certification as a health and wellness coach, to helping women grow into who they already are.

Questions

Before you book

What happens on the free possibilities call?

We talk about what's going on for you, what you'd like to be different, and what's been getting in the way. If coaching looks like a good fit, Lauren will suggest the option that suits you. If it isn't, she'll tell you, and point you to something that might help.

How is coaching different from therapy?

Therapy often focuses on healing the past. Coaching focuses on where you are now and where you want to go, with practical steps and accountability. Lauren's background as a psychologist means she understands the patterns behind self-doubt, but coaching is not a substitute for mental health treatment.

Is coaching in person or online?

All one-on-one coaching is by video, so you can work with Lauren from anywhere.

How long until I notice a difference?

Many clients feel lighter after the first few sessions. Lasting change in mindset and habits takes longer, which is why packages start at three months.

How much does coaching cost?

It depends on the option you choose. Lauren shares the investment on your free call, once you both know which option fits.
