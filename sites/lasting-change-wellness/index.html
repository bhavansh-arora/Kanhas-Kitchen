(04) — The S.H.I.N.E. Method

Five steps to a self-trust that lasts.

Built on Lauren's training as a psychologist and family therapist. Keep scrolling, and watch the room get brighter.

Scroll →
Step 01 / 05See
S

See yourself

Accept your whole self with compassion. Your strengths and quirks make you uniquely you.

What shifts

You stop arguing with who you are, and start working with it.

Step 02 / 05Honor
H

Honor yourself

Care for body, mind and spirit, with an optimistic outlook and habits that last.

What shifts

Taking care of yourself stops being the first thing you drop.

Step 03 / 05Investigate
I

Investigate the possibilities

Get curious about what makes you shine, and what a life you love would look like.

What shifts

A clear picture of what you're working toward, and why.

Step 04 / 05Neutralize
N

Neutralize the negative

Limit what dims your light or drains your energy, and release the beliefs that keep you small.

What shifts

Less energy goes to what drains you, and more goes to you.

Step 05 / 05Elevate
E

Elevate your energy

Lead with love, and become a source of positive energy for yourself and others.

What shifts

Confidence shows up on its own, without forcing it.

Now you're shining.
