About Lauren

Hi, I'm Lauren. You are always becoming.

Former psychologist and family therapist. National Board-Certified Health & Wellness Coach. Speaker, co-author and the creator of S.H.I.N.E. and Expect Amazing. Above all, a woman who rebuilt her own life at 53, and now helps others do the same.

For years, I hid behind a mask that looked confident. I put everyone else first, quietly doubted whether I deserved success, and called it being responsible.

At 53, I decided to make myself a priority. I started saying yes to what I love and stopped letting self-doubt make my decisions. I began treating myself with respect, in body, mind, heart and spirit. And as I released the beliefs that had kept me small, something unexpected happened: people noticed.

“You are a light in this world.”

What a friend told me, and the moment I knew I had to share this work

That shift became S.H.I.N.E. and the Expect Amazing approach. Behind it is decades of training, at the University of Pennsylvania as a psychologist and at the Philadelphia Child Guidance Clinic as a family therapist, and later as a board-certified health and wellness coach.

Helping others take control of their lives is my purpose. My work isn't about turning you into someone new or better. It's about helping you grow into who you already are, and giving you the tools to keep growing long after we finish.
