(01) — My storyIn Lauren's words

Lauren's story

For years I hid behind a mask that looked confident. At 53, I decided to make myself a priority. I said yes to what I love, quieted self-doubt, and people noticed. One friend told me, “You are a light in this world.”

(02) — Why I coach

That shift became S.H.I.N.E. and the Expect Amazing approach. Behind it is decades of training: at the University of Pennsylvania as a psychologist, and at the Philadelphia Child Guidance Clinic as a family therapist.

Helping others take control of their lives is my purpose. This work isn't about turning you into someone new or better. It's about helping you grow into who you already are, and giving you the tools to keep growing long after we finish.

Outside of coaching, I've spent more than 20 years as a volunteer leader in education, wellness fundraising, and parent and teacher support.
