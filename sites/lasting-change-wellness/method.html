The S.H.I.N.E. Method

Five steps to a self-trust
that lasts.

Most change fades because insight never becomes habit. S.H.I.N.E. is the framework Lauren built from her training as a psychologist and family therapist, and from her own reinvention at 53. It turns what you learn about yourself into the way you live.

Book a free Power Up call Walk through the steps

Why most change doesn't last

You don't need more insight. You need it to stick.

You've probably read the books and had the breakthroughs. Then real life came back, and so did the old patterns. S.H.I.N.E. is designed for the weeks after the breakthrough.

1

It starts with how you see yourself

Lasting change rarely comes from forcing new behaviour. It comes from changing the story you tell about who you are.

2

It's built into your days

Every session ends with a written summary, an action plan and custom homework, so the work keeps going between calls.

3

It's given the time it needs

New mindsets and habits take around three months to cement. That's why coaching packages start there.

S H I N E
S

Step 01 of 05

See yourself

Accept your whole self with compassion. Your strengths and your quirks are exactly what make you uniquely you.

In our sessions
We look at the stories you tell about yourself, where they came from, and which ones are still true.
What shifts
You stop arguing with who you are and start working with it.

A question to sit withWhat would you say to a friend who spoke to herself the way you speak to you?

H

Step 02 of 05

Honor yourself

Care for your body, mind and spirit. Build an optimistic outlook and healthy habits that hold up in real life.

In our sessions
We design small, realistic routines that protect your energy, and plan for the weeks when life gets in the way.
What shifts
Taking care of yourself stops being the first thing you drop.

A question to sit withWhat is one promise to yourself you could keep this week, no matter what?

I

Step 03 of 05

Investigate the possibilities

Get curious. What makes you shine? What do you want more of? What would a life you love actually look like?

In our sessions
We get specific about the goals you've been shelving, and what “amazing” would mean for you.
What shifts
You have a clear picture of what you're working toward, and why it matters.

A question to sit withIf you knew you couldn't fail, what would you start this year?

N

Step 04 of 05

Neutralize the negative

Limit the people and things that dim your light or drain your energy, and release the beliefs that keep you small.

In our sessions
We set boundaries you can actually keep, at work and at home, and practise the conversations that go with them.
What shifts
Less of your energy goes to what drains you, and more goes to you.

A question to sit withWhere are you saying yes when your whole body is saying no?

E

Step 05 of 05

Elevate your energy

Lead with love, and become a source of positive energy for yourself and for the people around you.

In our sessions
We turn your new mindsets into daily practice, so they hold up under pressure.
What shifts
Confidence shows up on its own, without forcing it.

A question to sit withWho do you become when you expect amazing of yourself?
