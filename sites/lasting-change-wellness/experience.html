(01) — BreatheOne minute

One breath can change the next hour.

Before any big decision, Lauren invites clients to arrive in the moment. Follow the light: breathe in as it grows, hold, and let go as it softens. Three slow breaths.

Ready?

(02) — Flip the switchFrom the Mindset Reset workbook

Notice it. Then flip it.

The thoughts that dim your light often sound like facts. Flip the switch to hear a kinder, truer version, then try it with a thought of your own.
