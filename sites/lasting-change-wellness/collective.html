The Expect Amazing Collective

A year-long personal leadership mastermind for women who refuse to settle for “good enough.” Release everything that no longer serves you, and discover who you can become when you expect amazing.

Book a Collective conversation What's inside

Why a collective

Growth is faster, braver and far more fun when you do it alongside women who get it. The Collective is a place to find your peers, build unshakeable self-trust, and lead your life and work with insight and intention.

What you'll build

A year to become your brightest self

The Collective blends Lauren's Expect Amazing framework, S.H.I.N.E. and the support of a committed group of women.

  • Self-trust that holds under pressure

    Grounded in Lauren's Architecture of Self-Trust: make decisions, keep commitments, set clear boundaries and grow visibly.

  • A circle of peers

    Women who value growth, cheer you on, challenge you kindly and celebrate your wins.

  • Deeper self-discovery

    Release the beliefs and habits that no longer serve you, and live more authentically.

  • Personal leadership

    Lead your life, your work and your relationships with clarity, confidence, wellbeing and joy.

Who it's for

For women who are serious about their growth

  • You want deeper self-discovery and to live more authentically
  • You value growth and want to be around others who do too
  • You don't want to settle for “good enough”
  • You lead a business, a team or a family, and want to lead yourself first
