Why a collective

The Collective is where you expand your foundation and find your people : women who speak the same language, value growth and exploration, and live with courage and conviction.

(02) — The foundationsYear-long mastermind

A foundational space to lead with unshakable self-trust.

“Everything you want is on the other side of what you trust yourself to deliver.” The Collective is built on four foundations.

01

Self-Worth

Know that you are worthy and deserving of everything you can imagine, and act like it.

02

Self-Trust

Build the internal foundation to make decisions, keep commitments and set clear boundaries.

03

Becoming

Release everything that no longer serves you, and grow into who you already are.

04

Body · Mind · Heart · Spirit

A whole-person approach, so the change shows up in how you feel, how you lead and how you live.
