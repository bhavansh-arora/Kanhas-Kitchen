(01) In George’s words

George’s story

“I’ve spent my life studying what makes champions. Not just in sport, but in leadership, business and life.”

Talent sets the floor. Leadership and culture set the ceiling.

From captaining championship hockey teams to leading corporate turnarounds, one truth has always stood out: great teams aren’t built on talent alone. They’re built on trust, standards and shared purpose.

Today George helps leaders, teams and organizations bridge the gap between potential and performance by building aligned leadership, resilient mindsets and championship cultures. Whether the room is a sports franchise, a corporate leadership team or an executive board, the focus is the same: build champions in mindset, leadership and culture.
