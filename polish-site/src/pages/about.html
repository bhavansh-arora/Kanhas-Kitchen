About Polish

Two decades of leadership work. One quiet place.

Meet Melissa

Senior executive coach,
facilitator & learning designer.

Melissa has spent over 20 years working with New Zealand business leaders on the human side of transformation — communication, culture, psychological safety and change readiness.

For twenty years that work happened in boardrooms, coaching sessions and workshops across the country. Coming from a finance background — and having founded and sold a cutting-edge tech business — she knows first-hand what it takes to lead through risk, growth and change.

She founded Polish to address the human side of AI transformation, and to give every leader access to the kind of strategic, grounded support that used to only exist in the room. Today she works with business chamber networks, RBP-eligible companies and individual organisations across New Zealand and Australia.
