How it works

A simple, considered
path forward.

No one-size-fits-all programme. We design around your context, then measure progress against what you set out to change.

  1. 01

    Discovery conversation

    Thirty relaxed minutes to understand where you are, what you want to change and whether we're the right fit. Complimentary and confidential.

    30 minutes · video, phone or in person
  2. 02

    Diagnose & design

    An in-depth first session mapping your situation against the nine pillars of human-centred leadership. Together we agree the two or three outcomes that matter most.

    Session one · 90 minutes
  3. 03

    Coach & apply

    Regular one-to-one sessions, each ending with something practical to try that week. Between sessions you have access to Melissa for the moments that can't wait.

    Fortnightly · 60 minutes
  4. 04

    Review & sustain

    We review progress against the outcomes we set — with your input and, where useful, your team's — and build the habits that keep it going after coaching ends.

    Mid-point and final reviews

Coaching questions

Before you
book.

Is coaching confidential?

Completely. What you discuss in coaching stays between you and Melissa. Where your organisation is funding coaching, we agree up front — with you — what (if anything) is shared, which is usually limited to attendance and the broad goals you've chosen.

Do we meet in person or online?

Whichever works best for you. Melissa coaches in person across Auckland and online for leaders elsewhere in New Zealand and Australia. Many leaders mix the two.

How is coaching different from your programmes?

Programmes build shared capability across a group. Coaching is entirely about you — your context, your decisions, your pace. Many organisations pair a Polish programme for the wider team with coaching for the senior leaders driving the change.

Can my organisation pay for coaching?

Yes — most coaching is funded by the leader's organisation, and we invoice directly. If you're not sure how to make the case internally, we can help you frame it on your discovery call.

What if it isn't the right fit?

Then we'll tell you, and point you to something that is. The discovery conversation is there so both of us can make a good decision — there's no obligation and no hard sell.
