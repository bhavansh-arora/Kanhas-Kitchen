01

A dedicated executive coaching page

Coaching is your highest-value, most personal offer — but today it's a single line under "Services". Leaders searching "executive coach Auckland" need a page that speaks to them directly.

Open the coaching page{{icon:arrowlink}}
What we built
Who it's for, the six things leaders bring to coaching, a four-step process, three engagement shapes, confidentiality FAQs and your own words on starting with "why".
Why it matters
A focused page ranks for coaching searches and answers the questions a CEO asks before they'll book — so the people who reach out are already a good fit.
02

A booking path that qualifies as it books

Four quick steps — focus, role & organisation size, time, details — before a discovery call lands in your diary.

Try the booking flow{{icon:arrowlink}}
What we built
A calendar showing Auckland time with the visitor's own time alongside (useful for Australian clients), a live summary, an "add to calendar" file and Google Calendar link on confirmation.
Why it matters
You see the person's role, organisation size and focus before the call, and there's no email back-and-forth to find a time. Ready to connect to your Google Calendar or Cal.com so availability is always real.
03

An AI leadership readiness check

Nine statements, one per pillar. Three minutes. An instant score, a radar profile, their biggest opportunity and a recommended next step.

Take the check{{icon:arrowlink}}
What we built
The result flows straight into the booking form — "Readiness check result attached · 58%" — so the discovery call starts where the leader actually is.
Why it matters
It turns visitors who aren't ready to "get in touch" into warm leads, and gives you a talking point that's uniquely yours. It's also a natural asset to share in chamber and RBP networks.
04

Your nine pillars, made visible

The framework is your intellectual property and your clearest differentiator — but on the current site it's mentioned, not shown.

See the approach page{{icon:arrowlink}}
What we built
An interactive dial on the home page and a full approach page with a reflective question for each pillar.
Why it matters
Seeing a considered framework builds authority before a single conversation. We drafted the pillar names from your published themes — we'd love to swap in your exact wording.
05

An RBP co-funding calculator

"50% co-funding" is a strong offer — showing the actual number for a team of twelve makes it real.

Try the calculator{{icon:arrowlink}}
What we built
Choose a programme, slide the number of participants, toggle RBP and GST, and see the investment per person and in total.
Why it matters
It removes price friction for HR and people leaders, and the team enquiry arrives with the programme and headcount already filled in.

See the speaking page{{icon:arrowlink}}

Why it matters
Better-shaped enquiries, faster replies, and a site that's ready to grow as new courses launch.
