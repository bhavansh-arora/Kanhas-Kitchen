Every talk is tailored to your audience and the moment your organisation is in. These are the starting points.

01
Keynote · 45–60 min

The gap isn't technical — it's human

Why the leaders who navigate AI well won't be the ones who understand the technology most — but the ones who lead with the most clarity, humanity and strategic intent.

Audiences leave with

  • A calmer, clearer view of what AI means for their leadership
  • The nine pillars as a practical lens on their own organisation
  • Three things to do differently on Monday
02
Keynote or workshop-keynote · 60–90 min

Leading people through change they didn't choose

Fear, resistance and rumour are normal. This talk gives leaders the language and the plan to replace them with direction — and to keep their people with them.

Audiences leave with

  • How to talk about AI change honestly and confidently
  • What psychological safety looks like in practice
  • A simple change-readiness conversation to run with their team
03
Keynote · 30–45 min

The importance of challenging traditions

Drawn from Melissa's own journey from finance to founding and selling a tech business: growth mindset, a healthier appetite for risk, and the power of co-petition over competition.

Audiences leave with

  • Where they're working "in" rather than "on" the business
  • A personal reframe on risk and failure
  • Ideas for collaborating with — not just competing against — their industry
