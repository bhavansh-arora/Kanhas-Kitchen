(01)Our story

Children achieve somethingspecial when theydance together.

That belief is where Dance Cart began. From humble beginnings in 2007, we started as a school-based program helping primary schools around Brisbane implement the new Australian Curriculum.

Our teachers are education specialists with experience in both the classroom and the dance studio. That mix matters: we know how schools run, how teachers plan and assess, and how to make a room full of children want to move.

Today, Dance Cart inspires more than 10,000 students a year. Our online programs reach schools in every corner of the country, remote or metro, large or very small, while our teachers still bring the energy in person to school halls, fete stages and graduation nights.
