**1. A course catalogue schools can serve themselves from**
Every program in one place (each Online Foundation band, the whole-school option, in-school terms, fete and graduation performances), filterable by year level and format, with the price on the page. Schools pick what they need and enrol, without the back-and-forth.

**2. Enrolment that matches how schools actually pay**
At checkout a school can pay by card, ask for an invoice for their finance office, or enter a purchase order number. In-school and event programs go through the same form as quote requests, so you get the school's details, numbers and preferred term in one go.

**3. The admin work done for you**
- Teachers book their 30 minute Zoom induction during enrolment and get a calendar invite.
- Schools can check fete and graduation date availability and request a date.
- A "small school" tick flags anyone who'd like to hear about grants and flexible pricing.
- A simple admin view shows every enrolment, payment status and upcoming induction, with a one-click export. There's a preview of it in the demo, linked from the footer.

**4. Small touches that lift bookings**
- A three-question "program finder" for teachers who aren't sure which band they need.
- When a school adds two or more bands, the cart suggests the whole-school program at $1,499, which saves them money and lifts your average order.
- A book-a-call scheduler and your free-resources sign-up on every page.

**How it works with your WordPress site**
You don't have to start again. There are two ways to do this:

- **Add to your current site.** The catalogue, enrolment form and checkout become new pages on dancecart.com.au. Card payments go through Stripe, and invoice and PO enrolments are recorded alongside them. Your lessons stay in Kajabi: when a school enrols, its teachers are given access to the right course automatically, so there's no manual adding.
- **Full refresh.** The new design you see in the preview, rebuilt on WordPress so you can still edit text, prices and programs yourself, with everything above built in.
